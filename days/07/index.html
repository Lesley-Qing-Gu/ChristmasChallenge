🎄 Shipmas · Day 7

Collie on the Wall

Three videos of a border collie on a bouldering wall, each made a different way. Watch them all, pick the one you like best, then find out how each was made.

A

Seedance

An AI video model, given a photo of the wall and one line: “Make a video of a border collie climbing here.” No real climbing went into it, so the dog moves the way the model imagines a dog would climb.

B

Me + Kling

This one is actually me. I filmed myself climbing this problem, then used Kling's motion control to swap me for a border collie. Every move is a real move on a real wall.

C

Claude

The same photo and the same one-line prompt. Claude has no video model, so it drew a cartoon collie and animated it in code on top of the photo.
