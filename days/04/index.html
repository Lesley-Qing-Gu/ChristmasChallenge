Key findings Det viktigaste

1The election dominates

The 13 September Riksdag election was the year's biggest topic, building from January onwards. The opposition bloc got 49.5% against 48.8% for the governing parties plus the Sweden Democrats. Forming a government has stalled: parliament rejected Magdalena Andersson as prime minister, and in November a övergångsregering (caretaker government) is still in charge.

2Children, school, young people

Mentioned in almost every episode. barn (child) is the second most frequent noun after Sverige; elev, ung person and sociala medier (social media) are common too.

3Money and work

Prices, wages, unemployment and budgets come up nearly every episode. Typical phrases: söka jobb (look for a job), miljoner kronor, varje månad.

4Gang crime

About three in four episodes mention police or crime. Frequent phrases: kriminella gäng (criminal gangs), begå brott (commit crimes), utsatta områden (vulnerable areas), grova brott (serious crimes).

5Migration policy

Peaks in summer (June to August), mainly about whether young people can keep their uppehållstillstånd (residence permit), plus svenskt medborgarskap (citizenship) and new SFI rules.

6Little foreign news

The show is mostly domestic news. Foreign coverage peaked in March with the US and Israeli strikes on Iran; Ukraine and the US get relatively little airtime.

Method Metod

  • Audio from the Sveriges Radio open API (programme ID 4916): 219 episodes, about 36 hours.
  • Transcribed locally with Whisper large-v3-turbo (mlx), language set to Swedish. Fixed intro/outro lines and Whisper hallucinations (such as “Svensktextning.nu”) were filtered out.
  • Topics are assigned per sentence by keyword, and a sentence can count for several topics, so the ranking shows relative attention, not an exact count of news items.
  • Word counts use simplemma lemmatisation plus manual fixes, with function words removed. säger (says) appears in almost every news item, so it was treated as a function word too.
