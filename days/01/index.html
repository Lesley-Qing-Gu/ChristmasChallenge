Day one ships the thing you're looking at: the calendar itself, and the plan behind it. 24 tiny products, one every two days, until Christmas Eve.

NOV 8 · SUN01

Why I'm doing this

01Every job search asks for end-to-end, hands-on delivery. Instead of describing it, I'm proving it: idea, design, build, ship. 24 times, in public.
02Things have felt a bit dull lately. I wanted something fun to look forward to every other day.
03Inspired by advent calendars: a small surprise behind every door. Here, each door is a working product.

The rules

24tiny products
2days per product
47days, Nov 8 → Dec 24
100%built in public

What shipped today

  • DESIGNA red advent calendar with 24 boxes, each with its own flat Christmas illustration.
  • INTERACTIONHover a box and it turns like a book page. Locked days only lift a corner. Tap twice on mobile.
  • MOTIONFloating pearls that follow your mouse, slow falling snow, and pearls that pop when clicked.
  • SYSTEMBoxes unlock on their date automatically, so each release is one line of config and a push.
  • STACKPlain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages. Source is open.
